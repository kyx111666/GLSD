# EXP-2C Analysis — Reference-Scale / Evidence-Scale Decoupling

## Protocol and verification

EXP-2C searched seven reference scales (`0.75, 1.0, 1.25, 1.5, 1.75,
2.0, 2.5`) while computing local evidence from the deterministic three-nearest
neighborhood of the selected reference. The mapping was generated by code using
`(absolute distance, smaller scale)` as the ordering key and was frozen in
`protocol.json`. The reference is present in every triplet.

The search budget is **210 configurations**:

`7 reference scales × 3 radii × 10 thresholds = 210`.

This equals EXP-2A's 210 configurations and is 2.33 times the baseline's 90.
No configuration was removed to reduce the budget. Canonical GLSD source files
were not modified. Exact replay passed for all 246 outer folds. The two fold-level
distribution files each contain 246 rows, and subject order matches EXP-2A for
both paired comparisons.

The paired intervals use 10,000 subject-level bootstrap resamples with seed 100.
They condition on the frozen curves and selected outer-fold configurations and
therefore do not represent end-to-end backbone-retraining uncertainty.

CAS(ME)3 largely preserves the EXP-2A reference choices: ME-TST remains exactly
at 1.25, while BoostingVRME selects 0.75 in 92/94 folds rather than all 94.
ME-TST/SAMMLV remains exactly at 2.0. BoostingVRME/SAMMLV does **not** retain its
EXP-2A choice of 1.0; it moves to 1.25 or 1.5 under the narrower local evidence.

## ME-TST/SAMMLV mechanism comparison

| Variant | TP | FP | FN | F1 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Baseline | 48 | 126 | 111 | 0.288288 |
| EXP-2A | 43 | 127 | 116 | 0.261398 |
| EXP-2C | 43 | 93 | 116 | 0.291525 |

EXP-2C recovers and slightly exceeds baseline F1, and it reduces FP by 33 versus
baseline and by 34 versus EXP-2A. It does **not** recover TP or FN: both remain at
the EXP-2A values (43 TP and 116 FN). Thus the repaired F1 is specifically an
FP-control effect from the local evidence neighborhood, not restoration of
sensitivity.

## Gate

**EXP2C_STRONG_CANDIDATE** under the predeclared Gate A:

1. No setting has a baseline-paired 95% CI entirely below zero.
2. Three of four point deltas versus baseline are positive.
3. Mean ΔF1 versus baseline is +0.004064.
4. One deterministic rule is used for all datasets and backbones.

This gate label identifies a candidate for further validation. It does not
promote EXP-2C to canonical GLSD or establish external generalization.

## Strict answers

1. **Does EXP-2C preserve EXP-2A's CAS(ME)3 gains?** Yes. Relative to baseline,
   ME-TST/CAS(ME)3 retains +0.010384 F1 with a fully positive CI, and
   BoostingVRME/CAS(ME)3 improves by +0.008642 with a fully positive CI. Against
   EXP-2A directly, the differences are small and both CIs cross zero.

2. **Does it repair the significant ME-TST/SAMMLV degradation?** Yes for F1:
   0.291525 is near and slightly above baseline 0.288288, and the gain over
   EXP-2A is +0.030127 with CI [0.016061, 0.047512]. Mechanistically, only FP is
   repaired; TP and FN are not.

3. **Is reference-scale expansion genuinely valuable?** It is valuable for
   CAS(ME)3: newly introduced scales dominate selection and both backbones beat
   baseline with positive CIs. It is not uniformly valuable: ME-TST/SAMMLV keeps
   2.0, while BoostingVRME/SAMMLV shifts to new scales but falls slightly below
   baseline and significantly below EXP-2A.

4. **Is evidence-scale expansion an important source of EXP-2A instability?**
   Yes for the key ME-TST/SAMMLV failure: narrowing evidence from seven scales to
   the fixed triplet removes the significant F1 loss without changing the chosen
   reference scale. Across all settings the conclusion is qualified rather than
   universal, because the same narrowing removes EXP-2A's BoostingVRME/SAMMLV
   gain and changes its selected reference.

5. **Is it worth entering the next validation round?** Yes. Gate A is met, the
   two CAS(ME)3 gains survive, and the major ME-TST/SAMMLV failure is repaired.
   The next validation should be prospectively fixed and should focus on the
   unresolved BoostingVRME/SAMMLV behavior. No EXP-2D or automatic canonical
   upgrade is performed here.
